What is Moneystep?

Moneystep turns everyday movement into a competition worth showing up for. Join a challenge, put your steps on the line, climb the Leaderboard, and earn real cash.

How do I win?

Each challenge has a set number of cash-paying spots. Finish high enough on the Leaderboard and you earn a cash payout, just like the winners on our Beta 1 Leaderboard.

How do you prevent unfair play?

We currently support only devices we trust to provide accurate step data, including iPhone, Apple Watch, Garmin, and Oura. We also monitor for unusual activity and investigate suspicious step patterns. Repeated violations may result in account suspension or removal.
